# Third-party notices

AISLOP's source and distribution archives do not bundle third-party source code
or binary libraries. Its runtime and development dependencies are installed as
separate distributions and remain subject to their own licenses; the dependency
declarations are in `pyproject.toml` and the resolved set is in
`requirements.lock`.

Accordingly, no third-party license text is required in AISLOP's release
artifacts at this time. This file must be updated before vendoring or bundling
third-party material. AISLOP itself is licensed under the MIT License in
`LICENSE`.
